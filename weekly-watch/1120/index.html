Macro and Rates. Next week's U.S. data calendar is light. Tuesday, Nov. 17 brings the ADP weekly employment change; Wednesday, Nov. 18 adds MBA mortgage applications and the EIA weekly crude-oil stocks print; Thursday, Nov. 19 carries initial and continuing jobless claims alongside a scheduled Fed Goolsbee speech. The recent backdrop stays rate-driven: CNBC reported on Oct. 28 that the Fed held rates steady, on Nov. 5 that the Nasdaq had dropped into correction territory, and on Nov. 4 that the 10-year Treasury yield had slid. The next dated policy and inflation markers all sit beyond this window - the Dec. 8-9 FOMC meeting, November CPI on Dec. 10 and November PPI on Dec. 15 - so the mid-week labour and housing readings, plus the weekly oil inventories, are the main domestic releases to watch.

U.S. Markets. Earnings take centre stage.
